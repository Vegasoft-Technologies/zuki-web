Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: 26 June 2026

This policy explains how Zuki's Caffetteria ("we", "us") looks after your privacy when you visit this website, and how we use cookies. We keep things simple, because this site is here to show you our café — not to collect data about you.

Who we are

Zuki's Caffetteria, 3B Queen Street, Exeter EX4 3SB, United Kingdom.
Phone: +44 1392 666999 · Instagram: @zukiscaffetteria

For any privacy question, just call us or message us on Instagram.

What we collect

This is a static information website. We do not run accounts, forms, or payments here, and we don't ask you to sign in. We don't collect your name, email, or payment details through this site.

If you place a food order or write a review, that happens on a third-party platform (Deliveroo, Just Eat or Tripadvisor) under their own privacy policies — not ours.

Cookies & similar storage

We use the smallest possible set:

  • Your cookie choice — when you choose "Accept all" or "Essential only", we remember that in your browser's local storage so we don't ask again. This never leaves your device.
  • Google Maps — the map showing our location loads from Google and may set Google cookies. It only loads after you accept it (either with "Accept all", or by clicking "Show map"). If you choose "Essential only", the map stays off.
  • Google Fonts — our typefaces are served by Google Fonts, which receives your browser's request to deliver them. No cookies are set for this.

You can change your mind any time by clearing this site's data in your browser, which will bring the cookie banner back.

Links to other sites

When you tap a button to Deliveroo, Just Eat, Tripadvisor or Instagram, you leave this website. We're not responsible for how those services handle your data — please read their own policies.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law (UK GDPR), you have rights over any personal data an organisation holds about you, including the right to access it, correct it, or have it deleted. As described above, this website itself doesn't hold personal data about you. If you've contacted us directly (by phone or Instagram) and want us to remove your details, get in touch and we'll sort it out.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the site works, we'll update this page and the "last updated" date above.
